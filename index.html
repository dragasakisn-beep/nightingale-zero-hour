  6. 145–170: executive board hearing

Adjudication principles

Require teams to articulate a decision, supporting evidence, accountable owner and consequence. Reveal follow-on injects based on the exercise conditions.

Do not equate a classroom score with certification eligibility. Review CMMC POA&M restrictions and contracting applicability against controlling sources before class.
